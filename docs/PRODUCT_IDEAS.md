# Product ideas (saved for later)

Check first: Whop must have a **$39.99 yearly plan** on Pocket Recon Plus (with the 7-day trial), or the app's "Yearly" button fails. The app finds it by price and billing period, or by `WHOP_PLAN_YEARLY`.

## Soon
1. **"Reto de 30 días" (30-day money challenge)**: one-time, about $9–$19. Clara guides a daily plan (a $500 emergency fund, cut one bill). A first purchase for people not ready to subscribe.
2. **Paid mini-courses** on Whop, about $19–$49, bilingual: credit from zero; first home with a VA loan; taxes for 1099 and cash workers.
3. **Printable budget planner**: bilingual PDF, about $5.

## Later
4. **Plus Familia**: shared household, about $7.99/month (needs shared households built).
5. **Pocket Recon for groups**: credit unions, nonprofits, veteran groups, employers; about $2–$3 per person per month.
6. **Community**: group chat + monthly live Q&A, about $3/month or included in Plus.
7. **1-on-1 sessions** with a certified financial coach (partner), about $49, revenue share.

## Don't
- Charge for help filing VA claims: only VA-accredited agents may charge, and not for initial claims. Keep VA tools free and point to free VSOs.
- Put affiliate offers inside Clara's answers: she never recommends specific banks, cards or lenders.
