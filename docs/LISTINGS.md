# Where Cuenta Clara is listed, and under what

How the app ranks what it is, everywhere:

1. Clara (AI) finance
2. Technology
3. Business (side hustles, gig work, self-employed pay)
4. Education
5. Military and veterans
6. Spanish / bilingual

Veterans and Spanish are features, not the headline. The App Store listing is in
`docs/APP_STORE.md`.

## Category labels to use

| Where | Main category | Also list under |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple App Store | Finance | Business (secondary) |
| Google Play | Finance | (tags) Budgeting, AI assistant |
| Product Hunt | Fintech | Artificial Intelligence, Productivity, Personal Finance |
| G2 / Capterra | Personal Finance Software | Business Finance Software, Productivity Software |
| Google / Meta ads | Finance: budgeting apps | Small business and self-employed, productivity tools |

**Business finance software:** yes, for freelancers, gig workers and side hustles
(pay that changes, tax set-aside, pay stub check). Don't claim invoicing, payroll or
accounting; the app doesn't do those.

**Productivity SaaS:** yes. Voice and receipt logging, reminders, the 3-month
forecast, and a monthly Plus subscription.

**CRM software:** not used. The app doesn't track customers or sales, and listing it
there would bring the wrong people and bad reviews. Revisit if a business mode with a
client list is ever built.
